Legend of Aethra
A full classic-style role-playing game, playable right on Torn

Summary

Legend of Aethra is a role-playing game inspired by the classic Final Fantasy games, built for Torn. A newly sworn Warden takes his first contract, to get a band of rebels into a reactor and out again, and falls into a story about stolen memories, four Crystals that hold up the sky, and the woman who wants all four.

It lives in the Legends folder on the Torn Arcade phone, next to its Soundtrack, and you can also open it from the Tampermonkey menu. It makes no requests of any kind and needs no API key. Like every game in Torn Arcade, it's completely free.

  • Five acts, about 40 hours: eight playable characters, each with their own story, and an ending that remembers who you brought along.
  • A world to explore: an open 3D world map you cross on foot, by talonbird, skimmer, boat, submersible and airship.
  • Active-time battles: 154 monsters and 52 bosses, Prisms to slot spells and skills, Surge Arts and Echo summons.
  • An original soundtrack: 45 tracks, with a theme for every town and music of its own for the big battles.

⚠️ Before You Install: Large Scripts

The game is about 2.2 MB and its optional Soundtrack about 9 MB, much bigger than a usual userscript. They can take a moment to install and to load, and some mobile browsers and userscript managers may not be able to install or run scripts this size. If the Soundtrack won't install or slows your phone down, leave it out: the game still plays, with sound effects but no music. A PC or laptop is the most reliable way to play.

Features

The Story

  • From the slums under Halcyon's plate to the Sky Citadel: reactors, a cursed forest, a desert colossus, an underwater sanctum and a broken sky.
  • Quiet nights at the inn where the party talks, a rival who keeps turning up, and a last night before the end.
  • Epilogues for every character who joined you.

Battles and Growth

  • Active-time battles in Wait or Active mode, with front and back rows, elements, status effects and back attacks.
  • Prisms: 61 of them, slotted into weapons and armour for magic, commands, support and Echo summons. Link them in pairs; they level up.
  • Surge Arts for every character, Mimic, a Bestiary, difficulty settings and New Game+.

A World to Explore

  • 300 maps: towns that each have their own shape, 157 interiors with upstairs rooms and cellars, and big multi-floor dungeons.
  • Some places can only be reached by sea or by air. Day turns to night, and the weather changes as you travel.
  • Hidden places and their guardians, a ghost ship, legendary Prisms and a dangerous little island where the bravest go to level up.

Side Content

  • The Arcana card game (66 cards), talonbird races and breeding, slots, a shooting gallery, snowboarding and a submarine duel.
  • The Battle Square, bounties, side quests, Echo trials, roaming Weapons, super-bosses and the 50-floor Abyssal Spire.

How to Install

  1. Install Tampermonkey for your browser.
  2. Open the Legend of Aethra install link and click Install (about 2.2 MB).
  3. Optional, but recommended: install the Soundtrack for the music (about 9 MB; see the warning above).
  4. Reload Torn and open the Legends folder on the Torn Arcade phone, or choose Play Legend of Aethra in the Tampermonkey menu.

It plays with a keyboard, or with the on-screen touch pad in Torn Arcade. Updates install automatically through Tampermonkey, and your saves are kept.

Controls

  • Arrows / WASD: move. Z, Enter or Space: OK. X or Esc: back. C: menu.
  • Shift: run. M: world map. Hold Q + E to flee a battle.

Scripting Rules

  • No requests: the game and the Soundtrack never contact any website, Torn's API included. Everything is built into the scripts.
  • Never touches Torn's page: it doesn't read, click or change anything in Torn. It only draws its own window.
  • No automation: it's a game in a window, nothing more.
  • Open source to read: no outside websites, no websockets and no hidden code. The full source is on GitHub.
